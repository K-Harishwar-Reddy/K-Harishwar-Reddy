About

Research at the intersection of AI and medicine

My primary interests are digital pathology, explainable foundation models, self-supervised learning, and multi-omics. I also have experience with vision-language models and large language models for multimodal biomedical AI and computer vision applications.

I build end-to-end systems for representation learning, segmentation, cell detection, multiple instance learning, pathomic feature extraction, clinical prediction, and model explainability.
